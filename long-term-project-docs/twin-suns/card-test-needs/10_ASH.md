# ASH: multiplayer test needs

120 of the 214 reviewed ASH cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### The Armorer — Steel Shapes Us #001

> Action [Exhaust]: Play an upgrade from your resources on a unit that entered play this phase (paying its cost). If you do, resource the top card of your deck. // When Attack Ends: You may play an upgrade from your resources on a friendly unit. If you do, resource the top card of your deck.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the upgrade is played only from your own resources and the resourced card comes from your own deck, never the teammate's.
- Ensure the deployed side can attach the upgrade to the teammate's unit as well as your own.

### Fennec Shand — Ready for War #002

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust, exhaust a friendly unit]: Play a unit from your hand (paying its cost). It enters play ready. // Saboteur / Action [1 resource, exhaust a friendly unit]: Play a unit from your hand. It enters play ready.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the cost "exhaust a friendly unit" can exhaust the teammate's unit as well as your own, on both sides.

### Baylan Skoll — Power Beyond Dream #003

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: Give a friendly unit +2/+2 for this phase if it's the only unit you control in its arena. // On Attack: You may give a friendly unit +2/+2 and Sentinel for this phase if it's the only non-leader unit you control in its arena. (Enemy units in its arena must attack a Sentinel when they attack you.)

**Always Two**
- Ensure a teammate's unit in the arena does not count as a unit you control for the "only unit you control in its arena" check.
- Ensure a teammate’s unit can be selected, but will not receive a buff.

### Grand Admiral Thrawn — Victory is Mine #004

> Action [Exhaust]: Attack with a unit. It gains Restore 2 for this attack if you control the same number of units as the defending player. // Restore 2 / On Attack: If you control more units than the defending player, you may defeat a non-leader unit they control.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure the unit-count comparison, for both Restore 2 and the On Attack condition, is made against the defending player only, not the other opponents.
- Ensure the defeated non-leader unit must be controlled by the defending player, not by another opponent.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the unit-count comparison counts only units you control (not the teammate's) against units controlled by the defending enemy only (not both enemies combined).
- Ensure the defeated non-leader unit must be controlled by the defending enemy, not the other enemy.
- Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit.
