# IC27: multiplayer test needs

84 of the 154 reviewed IC27 cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Darth Vader — No One to Stop Us #001

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust, defeat a friendly unit]: Draw a card and heal 2 damage from your base. // On Attack: You may defeat another friendly unit. If you do, draw a card and heal 2 damage from your base.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the teammate's unit can be chosen to defeat, both for the Action's cost and for the On Attack ability.
- Ensure the card draw and the heal go to you and your own base, not the teammate's.

### Mace Windu — Here With Backup #004

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: Attack with a unit. It gains Restore 2 for this attack. (When it attacks, heal 2 damage from your base.) // Restore 2 / When Deployed: Resource the top card of your deck.

### Luke Skywalker — I Have to Face Him #006
