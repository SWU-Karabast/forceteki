# LOF: multiplayer test needs

96 of the 201 reviewed LOF cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Ahsoka Tano — Fighting For Peace #003

> Action [Exhaust, use the Force (lose your Force token)]: Give a friendly unit Sentinel for this phase. (Enemy units in its arena must attack a sentinel when they attack you.) // On Attack: You may give a friendly unit Sentinel for this phase.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the choice can be the teammate's unit as well as your own (leader Action and deployed On Attack).

### Kanan Jarrus — Help Us Survive #004

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: Give a Shield token to a Creature or Spectre unit. // Shielded (When you deploy this leader, give a Shield token to him.) / While you control another Creature or Spectre unit, this unit gets +2/+2.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the deployed +2/+2 is enabled only by another Creature or Spectre unit you control; a teammate's does not count.

### Morgan Elsbeth — Following the Call #005

> Action [Exhaust]: Choose a friendly unit that attacked this phase. Play a unit from your hand that shares a keyword with the chosen unit. It costs 1 resource less. // On Attack: The next unit you play this phase costs 1 resource less if it shares a keyword with a friendly unit.

### Supreme Leader Snoke — In the Seat of Power #006

### The Will of the Force #227

> Return a non-leader unit to its owner's hand. You may use the Force (lose your Force token). If you do, that player discards a random card from their hand.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure that when one opponent controls a unit another opponent owns, the unit returns to its owner's hand and the owner (not the controller) discards a random card from their own hand after the Force is used.
- Ensure the Force token lost is the playing player's own, and the owner discards only when it was lost; if the playing player has no Force token, the unit still returns and nobody discards.

**Always Two**
- Ensure that returning the teammate's unit makes the teammate (the owner) discard a random card from their own hand, and the Force token lost is the playing player's, never the teammate's.
- Ensure that when an enemy controls a unit the teammate owns (or the teammate controls a unit an enemy owns), the owner discards a random card from their hand, not the controller.
- Ensure that when an enemy's unit is returned to hand, that enemy discards a random card from their own hand and the other enemy and the teammate do not discard.
