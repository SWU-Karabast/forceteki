# SEC: multiplayer test needs

143 of the 220 reviewed SEC cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Chancellor Palpatine — How Liberty Dies #001

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: Search the top 5 cards of your deck for a card with Plot, reveal it, and draw it. (Put the other cards on the bottom of your deck in a random order.) // When Deployed: The next card you play using Plot this phase costs 3 resources less.

**Always Two**
- Ensure a Plot card the teammate plays neither benefits from nor uses up the discount.

### Jabba the Hutt — Wonderful Human Being #002

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: A friendly damaged unit deals 1 damage to an enemy unit. If the friendly unit has 3 or more damage on it, it deals 2 damage instead. // When another friendly unit is dealt damage and survives: You may have that unit deal that much damage to an enemy unit.
