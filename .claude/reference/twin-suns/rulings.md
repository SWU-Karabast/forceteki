# Judge rulings and deductions (Twin Suns and Always Two)

Only what the official rules do not say. The Comprehensive Rules v9.0 (sections 11 and 12 for Twin Suns) and the official Doubles overview are not repeated here; anything they state is left out. Each row is either a **judge ruling** (decided by Anthony) or **deduced** (a reading that follows from the official text but is not stated). Rows marked "to confirm" are not confirmed by an official source.

The IDs (R1, R2, ...) are stable and used by the engine test notes; gaps are rulings that turned out to be official or were merged into another row.

| ID | Topic | Ruling | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Static condition saying "an opponent" | A static condition or comparison that says "an opponent" (no choice step) is true if at least one opponent meets it; no opponent is chosen. This differs from abilities that choose an opponent when used. [Corrupt Politician, Dedra Meero] | deduced |
| R2 | Same condition with two enemies (Always Two) | In Always Two the R1 reading applies per enemy: either enemy qualifying is enough, nothing is summed across the two enemies, and the teammate is never an opponent. | deduced |
| R3 | Reveal, look at, disclose (Always Two) | A reveal is seen by everyone including the teammate. Disclose uses only your own hand and aspects; a teammate's hand or aspects never count. | deduced |
| R7 | "The player to their right" (Twin Suns) | "The player to their right" is the player before them in turn order (the counterclockwise neighbour), since turns pass clockwise. | deduced |
| R8 | Seating and "player to their right" (Always Two) | Teams sit across from each other, so teammates are adjacent. "The player to their right" is a teammate for one player on each team and an enemy for the other. Seat order within a team is not defined. | deduced (to confirm) |
| R9 | Choices made in turn by each player | Player order runs clockwise. Where each player chooses and the card gives no start, it begins with the player who played the card (active player); "in player order" begins with the player to the active player's left. In Always Two this follows the seating in R8. | deduced (to confirm) |
| R10 | Base damage at or past health (Always Two) | A player whose base is at or past its health is not eliminated and can still do things that put more damage on their own base, such as paying "deal damage to your base" costs. [Saw Gerrera] | deduced |
| R13 | "If you have done X this phase" (Always Two) | A condition about what you did this phase counts only your own actions and plays, never the teammate's. | deduced |
| R14 | Opponent's nth card/action counted per player | A count of "an opponent's" nth card or action is kept per individual enemy player, not per team. [Ki-Adi-Mundi - Composed and Confident] | deduced |
| R15 | Exploit with teammate's units | Exploit can only defeat the player's own units, never a teammate's, even though they are friendly. | deduced (to confirm) |
| R16 | "Their previous action" | "Their" means that opponent's own most recent action this phase, even if others acted in between. In Always Two it is per individual enemy, not the team. [Fully Armed and Operational] | deduced |
| R17 | Comparisons over "enemy units" | A comparison or total over "enemy units" pools all opponents' units together; it is not evaluated per opponent. [Dengar - Take Your Shot] | deduced |
| R18 | "If [condition] an opponent, [effect on] that opponent" | The controller chooses an opponent for whom the condition is true and the effect applies to that opponent; in Always Two only enemies can be chosen. [Air Superiority] | deduced |
| R19 | Restore heals controller's base | Restore heals the base of the controller of the unit that has Restore, however it was gained; a teammate's unit with granted Restore heals the teammate's base. [Home One] | deduced (to confirm) |
| R20 | "You" in a granted ability | In any ability or keyword granted to a card, in either format, "you" is the controller of the card that has the ability, including abilities a Pilot grants to its Vehicle even if a teammate controls the Pilot. [General Krell - Heartless Tactician, Asajj Ventress - I Work Alone] | judge ruling |
| R21 | "Friendly resource" includes teammate's | The teammate's resources are friendly resources: they gain granted abilities (the teammate uses a gained Smuggle with their own resources) and can be chosen by effects acting on a "friendly resource". [Tech - Source of Insight, San Hill] | deduced |
| R22 | Exchanging control with a teammate's unit | Each unit goes to the other unit's former controller, so the enemy unit goes to the teammate, not to the player who played the event; Credit tokens from Doublecross go to whoever takes the lower-cost unit. [Choose Sides, Doublecross] | deduced |
| R23 | Friendly Credit tokens | A teammate's Credit tokens are friendly Credit tokens in every use (counted, and defeatable as a cost or effect). Ruled for Credit tokens only; other friendly tokens are not covered. [Backed by the Hutts, Cad Bane - Now It's My Turn] | judge ruling |
| R24 | "Friendly" includes teammate's cards (general) | Most effects, conditions and costs that say "friendly" apply to the teammate's cards too, including costs that exhaust, defeat or return a friendly card. Exceptions: playing a teammate's cards, attacking with their units, paying with their resources, and Exploit. [Disturbance in the Force, Emperor Palpatine] | judge ruling |
| R25 | Using a teammate's unit's ability through your own card | When your card lets you use a teammate unit's When Defeated (or similar) ability, "you" in that ability is you, the controller of the card using it. Only a card's controller can use its own action abilities. Opposite of R20. [Chimaera - Reinforcing the Center, Shadow Caster - Just Business] | deduced |
| R28 | Base at or past its health | A base at or past its health has remaining HP 0 (never negative), so effects using remaining HP see 0; damage can still be dealt to it and "deals combat damage to a base" abilities still trigger. [Supremacy - Destroy the Resistance, Blockade Runner] | deduced (to confirm) |
| R29 | Choosing several "enemy" cards | When a card has you reveal, look at or choose several enemy cards, they can come from any combination of opponents/enemies unless the card says one player; "its controller" is each card's own controller. [Scanning Officer, Elia Kane - False Convert] | deduced |
| R30 | Choose any number of players/opponents/bases | "Any number" allows any subset (none, one, several, all legal choices, including the teammate in Always Two), and each chosen target is affected once; an exact count ("choose 2 players") works the same for any N legal players. Engine requirement: the engine does not yet support arbitrary player subsets, so both formats need full support and tests (Yoda - Old Master, Chio Fain). | deduced |
| R33 | "another unit"/"a unit" no control qualifier: no note | A card choosing "a unit" or "another unit" with no control qualifier needs no format-specific note, since the choice is open to any unit as in 1v1 (Hera Syndulla - Spectre Two). A note is needed only when the text names who controls the card or involves players or bases. | judge ruling |
| R34 | Always Two: upgrades and discounts | An upgrade can be played on any unit, including the teammate's, unless the card restricts it ("Attach to a friendly unit"). A discount applies to whoever plays the card unless the text limits it to its controller (Guardian of the Whills: "the first unit YOU play"). | judge ruling |
| R35 | Choosing "an enemy unit" | In Twin Suns the phrase "enemy unit" needs no note on each card (any opponent's unit). In Always Two only an enemy's unit can be chosen, never the teammate's, and every card with a chosen enemy unit keeps that line. | judge ruling |
| R36 | Always Two: Pilots | A Pilot (or a deployed Piloting leader) can be attached to a teammate's Vehicle; the player who plays or deploys it controls the upgrade and the Vehicle keeps its controller. Abilities granted to the Vehicle use the Vehicle's controller as "you"; the Pilot's own text uses the Pilot's controller. | judge ruling |
| R39 | Effect that finishes a player's base | If an effect eliminates a player partway through, the rest of the effect that refers to that player's cards mostly fizzles because those cards are gone (Maul cannot take a unit of the player whose base he just finished). Exceptions are reviewed card by card. | deduced |
| R40 | "An opponent" in a condition of a resolved effect | In a resolved effect (When Played, On Attack, event) that checks "an opponent", the controller chooses which opponent (Always Two: which enemy) the condition is checked against and may pick one for whom it is not met. Believed, not officially confirmed; a repeated "an opponent" in separate sentences is a separate choice each time (R55). | judge ruling |
| R41 | Who collects a Bounty | A Bounty is collected by an opponent of the unit's controller; if the controller (or, in Always Two, the controller's teammate) defeats the unit, the defeating player chooses which opponent/enemy collects it. Believed, not confirmed; test once under the keyword. | judge ruling |
| R42 | Card moved to owner's discard by elimination | A card the eliminated player controls but does not own goes to its owner's discard pile without being defeated or leaving play (CR 11.3.1), so no When Defeated, Bounty or leaves-play trigger fires, except an effect lasting "until it leaves play" (Galen Erso, Regional Governor), which ends. "For this phase" or regroup-return effects simply end (Change of Heart). | judge ruling |
| R44 | Who resources a defeated card | A When Defeated ability that puts the defeated card into a resource zone (Expendable Mercenary) is resolved by the player who controlled the unit when defeated, and the card enters that player's resource zone, whoever owns it. | judge ruling |
| R45 | "An opponent" in a constant ability | A constant ability that mentions "an opponent" involves no choice: it applies while any one opponent (Always Two: either enemy) meets the condition, each checked individually. The official rulings that the controller chooses apply to triggered abilities like Broken Horn, not constant ones. | judge ruling |
| R46 | Always Two: acting on a teammate's resource | A card saying "friendly resource" can choose, defeat or act on the teammate's resource as on the player's own, including as a cost or downside. A player can never spend a teammate's resources to pay costs. | judge ruling |
| R47 | Always Two: using a teammate's card or ability | An effect that lets you use, play or choose a friendly card or ability can use the teammate's (Fire Across the Galaxy with a teammate's Spectre, Dryden Vos with a teammate-owned captured card). Working lean: the player using the effect resolves it, and unclear cases are decided card by card; "you" counting conditions count only what you did. A teammate's ability counts as a friendly ability for Ty Yorrick (the controller of Ty decides the +1); whether a teammate's heal counts for Darth Sidious is open. | judge ruling |
| R48 | Losing the game by a card ability | A card that says a player loses the game (Final Showdown) eliminates that player as a defeated base does (owned cards removed, others' cards to owners' discard), but no player heals because nobody eliminated them. The game then ends at the end of the phase and the most remaining base HP wins (CR 11.7.1). | judge ruling |
| R49 | Winning the game by a card ability | A card that says a player wins the game (Confidence in Victory) ends the game immediately with that player winning and everyone else losing; the end-of-phase and most-base-HP rule does not apply. | judge ruling |
| R50 | Always Two: winning or losing by a card ability | A card-effect loss applies to the whole team (Final Showdown). "Play only as your first action" (Confidence in Victory) means the player's own first action, not the team's. A teammate's units in the chosen arena also block "only player who controls units". The win-for-both rule is official (Doubles: if one teammate wins via a card ability, they both win). | judge ruling |
| R51 | Unqualified wording means no restriction | When card text names no qualifier ("a resource", "a unit", "a base", "a player"), the choice is unrestricted: any such object in play, whoever controls or owns it, including the teammate's and every opponent's (Han Solo, Never Tell Me the Odds). Write a note only for what the card does beyond that, such as a remaining-HP condition or the base's controller drawing. | judge ruling |
| R52 | Always Two: attaching a teammate's Pilot with an effect | When an effect (Corvus) attaches the teammate's Pilot to your Vehicle, the teammate still controls the upgrade, as the effect does not take control. Text granted to the Vehicle uses the Vehicle's controller as "you"/"enemy", while the Pilot's own text (Luke Skywalker, Hero of Yavin) uses the Pilot's controller; read each Pilot individually. | judge ruling |
| R54 | "That player" after returning a card to owner's hand | After a card says to return a unit to its owner's hand and then "that player" acts (The Will of the Force), "that player" is the owner, not the controller, when they differ. | judge ruling |
| R55 | "An opponent" repeated in separate sentences | When one triggered ability says "an opponent" in two separate sentences (Broken Horn), the player chooses an opponent separately for each, and they may be different players (never the teammate in Always Two). | judge ruling |
| R56 | Always Two: teammate's Credit token for own costs | A player cannot defeat their teammate's Credit token to pay 1 less on their own cost, since the token's text is used by its controller; it is still a friendly Credit token for cards that count or defeat Credit tokens (R23). | judge ruling |
| R57 | "Choose a unit controlled by each player" | An effect that has the controller choose a unit controlled by each player (Impropriety Among Thieves) needs a legal unit from every player still in the game; if any player cannot meet it, the event fizzles and nothing changes control. | judge ruling |
