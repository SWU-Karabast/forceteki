# IBH: multiplayer test needs

14 of the 23 reviewed IBH cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Leia Organa — Get to Your Transports! #001

> Action [1 resource, Exhaust]: Heal 1 damage from a friendly unit. // On Attack: Heal 1 damage from a friendly unit and 1 damage from another friendly unit.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the choice can be the teammate's unit as well as your own, for the leader-side heal and for both deployed heals.

### I'll Cover For You #005

> Deal 1 damage to an enemy unit and 1 damage to another enemy unit.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure the two different enemy units can belong to the same opponent or to two different opponents.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the two different enemy units can belong to the same enemy or to both enemies.
- Ensure only an enemy's unit can be chosen, never the teammate's.

### I've Found Them #009

> Reveal the top 3 cards of your deck. Draw a unit revealed this way, then discard the other revealed cards.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the revealed top 3 cards come only from your own deck.

### R2-D2 — Known to Make Mistakes #011

> On Attack: If you control a Command unit, exhaust an enemy ground unit that costs 4 or less.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure the exhaust target can be an eligible enemy ground unit of any opponent.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the teammate's Command unit does not satisfy "if you control a Command unit."
- Ensure only an enemy's ground unit can be chosen, never the teammate's.

### Tauntaun Mount #015

> When Defeated: Heal 2 damage from your base.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the 2 damage is healed from the base of the unit's controller, not the teammate's base.

### Go for the Legs #018

> Exhaust an enemy ground unit.

**Always Two**
- Ensure only an enemy's unit can be chosen, never the teammate's.

### C-3PO — Oh Dear, Oh Dear #019

> When Played: If you control a Cunning unit, draw a card.

**Always Two**
- Ensure a Cunning unit controlled by the teammate does not enable the draw.

### Improvised Detonation #021

> Attack with a unit. It gets +2/+0 for this attack. (You can only attack with a ready friendly unit.)

**Always Two**
- Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit.

### General Rieekan — Stalwart Tactician #023

> Action [Exhaust]: Attack with another Heroism unit. It gets +2/+0 for this attack.  (You can only attack with a ready friendly unit.)

**Always Two**
- Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit.

### Watch This #052

> Return a non-leader unit that costs 6 or less to its owner's hand. Exhaust each other enemy unit in the same arena.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure every other unit in that arena controlled by any opponent is exhausted, regardless of which opponent controls it.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the other units of both enemies in that arena are exhausted, and the teammate's units are not.

### Hoth Lieutenant #064

> When Played: You may attack with another unit. It gets +2/+0 for this attack.  (You can only attack with a ready friendly unit.)

**Always Two**
- Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit.

### Admiral Ozzel — As Clumsy as He Is Stupid #082

> When Defeated: Each opponent discards a card from their hand.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure every opponent discards a card from their own hand, each choosing their own card.

**Always Two**
- Ensure both enemy players discard a card from their hand and the teammate does not.

### You Have Failed Me #095

> Defeat a friendly unit. If you do, ready a friendly unit with 5 or less power.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the choice can be the teammate's unit as well as your own, both for the unit defeated and for the unit readied.

### The Desolation of Hoth #104

> Defeat up to 2 enemy units that each cost 3 or less.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure the enemy units can be chosen from any opponents' units, including two units of different opponents.

**Always Two**
- Ensure only enemies' units can be chosen, never the teammate's, and the two can belong to different enemies.
