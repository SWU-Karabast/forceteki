# LAW: multiplayer test needs

120 of the 208 reviewed LAW cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Saw Gerrera — Bring Down the Empire #001

> Action [Exhaust]: Attack with a unit. It gets +2/+0 and gains Overwhelm for this attack. After completing this attack, defeat it. // When Attack Ends: If this unit survived, you may attack with another unit. It gets +2/+0 and gains Overwhelm for this attack. After completing this attack, defeat it.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit.

### Tobias Beckett — People are Predictable #002

> Action [Exhaust]: Choose a friendly unit. An opponent takes control of it. If they do, create a Credit token. // When Deployed: Defeat any number of units you own but don't control. For each unit defeated this way, create a Credit token and draw a card.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure the controller chooses which opponent takes control of the chosen friendly unit, and the choice persists through the ability.
- Ensure Deployed can defeat units the controller owns that any opponent controls.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the choice of friendly unit can be the teammate's unit as well as your own.
- Ensure the choice of opponent is either enemy, never the teammate.
- Ensure that units owned by your teammate but given away via Tobias are not defeated when he is deployed.
