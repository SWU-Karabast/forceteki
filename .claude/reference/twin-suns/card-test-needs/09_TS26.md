# TS26: multiplayer test needs

60 of the 78 reviewed TS26 cards need Twin Suns or Always Two tests beyond the engine-level ones (see ../engine-test-notes.md). Cards not listed need none.

### Count Dooku — Offering Aid #001

> Action [Exhaust]: Choose 2 players. They each heal 1 damage from their base and create a Battle Droid token. // Restore 2 (When this unit attacks, heal 2 damage from your base.) / On Attack: Create 2 Battle Droid tokens.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure any 2 non-eliminated players can be chosen, including the controller; each heals their own base and creates a Battle Droid token under their own control.

**Always Two**
- Ensure any 2 players can be chosen: enemies, the teammate or the controller.
- Ensure each chosen player heals their own base and creates the Battle Droid token under their own control (the teammate gets their own token).

### Anakin Skywalker — Protect Her At All Costs #002

> Action [Exhaust]: If 2 or more friendly units entered play this phase (including tokens and leaders), give a Shield token to 1 of them. // Sentinel (Enemy units in this arena must attack a Sentinel when they attack you.) / On Attack: Give a Shield token to another friendly unit that entered play this phase.

**Twin Suns**
- Ensure a unit that entered play from either of the controller's two leaders (a deployed leader) counts toward the 2 friendly units on the leader-side ability and can receive the Shield token.

**Always Two**
- Ensure the leader-side ability is enabled when one friendly unit you played and one the teammate played entered play this phase, and when both were played by the teammate.
- Ensure the Shield token from the leader-side ability can be given to the teammate's unit that entered play this phase.
- Ensure the deployed On Attack can give the Shield token to the teammate's unit that entered play this phase, while a teammate's unit that did not enter play this phase cannot be chosen.

### Padmé Amidala — Follow My Lead #004
