# 07_TS26 (TS26) card review

84 cards, in set order. Review against the official card text (`test/json/Card/`), not our implementations.

### 001 - Count Dooku - Offering Aid

- Internal name: `count-dooku#offering-aid`
- Type: Leader
- Text: Action [Exhaust]: Choose 2 players. They each heal 1 damage from their base and create a Battle Droid token.
- Deployed: Restore 2 (When this unit attacks, heal 2 damage from your base.) On Attack: Create 2 Battle Droid tokens.
- Status: Unreviewed

### 002 - Anakin Skywalker - Protect Her At All Costs

- Internal name: `anakin-skywalker#protect-her-at-all-costs`
- Type: Leader
- Text: Action [Exhaust]: If 2 or more friendly units entered play this phase (including tokens and leaders), give a Shield token to 1 of them.
- Deployed: Sentinel (Enemy units in this arena must attack a Sentinel when they attack you.) On Attack: Give a Shield token to another friendly unit that entered play this phase.
- Rules: Anakin’s leader ability still can be used as an action even if fewer than 2 friendly units entered play this phase (but no Shield token is created).
- Status: Unreviewed

### 003 - Maul - Collective Ambition

- Internal name: `maul#collective-ambition`
- Type: Leader
- Text: Action [Exhaust]: Choose a unit. If it has more different keywords than it has Experience tokens on it, give an Experience token to it and deal 1 damage to it.
- Deployed: When Deployed/On Attack: Choose a unit. If it has more different keywords than it has Experience tokens on it, give an Experience token to it and deal 1 damage to it.
- Rules: Multiple instances of the same keyword aren't considered different keywords, including for stacking keywords like Raid X. The chosen unit gets the Experience token and is dealt 1 damage simultaneously, so it will survive if it has 1 remaining HP.
- Status: Unreviewed

### 004 - Padmé Amidala - Follow My Lead

- Internal name: `padme-amidala#follow-my-lead`
- Type: Leader
- Text: Action [Exhaust]: If 2 or more friendly units entered play this phase (including tokens and leaders) , attack with 1 of them, even if it's exhausted. It can't attack bases for this attack.
- Deployed: When Attack Ends: You may attack with another friendly unit that entered play this phase, even if it's exhausted. It can't attack bases for this attack.
- Rules: Padme’s leader ability still can be used as an action even if fewer than 2 friendly units entered play this phase (but you don't get to attack). If you use Padme’s leader ability, you must attack with a unit, if able. "When Attack Ends" abilities trigger at the same time the attacking unit deals combat damage. Padme does not need to survive the attack in order to attack with another unit. Units that can’t attack bases can still damage bases through other abilities, like Overwhelm.
- Status: Unreviewed

### 005 - Savage Opress - You Must Have Your Revenge

- Internal name: `savage-opress#you-must-have-your-revenge`
- Type: Leader
- Text: Each friendly unit with the most power among friendly units gains Overwhelm. (When attacking an enemy unit, deal excess damage to the opponent's base.)
- Deployed: Raid 3 (This unit gets +3/+0 while attacking.) Overwhelm Each other friendly unit gains Overwhelm.
- Rules: Shields prevent all damage from a unit with Overwhelm. There is no excess damage since the defender is not defeated. If the defender is defeated during an attack before combat damage is dealt, all combat damage is considered excess damage for Overwhelm. Abilities that refer to a card’s power include temporary modifiers. If multiple friendly units are tied for the most power, they each gain Overwhelm.
- Status: Unreviewed
