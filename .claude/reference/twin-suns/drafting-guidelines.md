# Guidelines for drafting multiplayer test notes

Every agent that drafts Twin Suns / Always Two notes for cards must read this file first. Add a line here whenever the reviewer gives a standing instruction, so it applies to every future batch.

## Scope: what a card note should (and should not) cover
- **Only describe what is specific to this card in the format.** If the card behaves as it does in 1v1, say so: "Ensure it behaves as in 1v1; no format-specific case."
- **Engine-level mechanics are tested once in the engine scenarios, not per card.** Do not write a note about them just because the card has the keyword or the effect: Overwhelm, Sentinel, Saboteur, the defending player, and the generic capture / rescue rules. Mention them only when this card does something unusual with one (for example, grants it conditionally in a way that depends on the number of opponents or on a teammate).
- Do not restate rules from the card's text. Write what a test must show, not what the card says.

## Cards not to draft
- Cards that depend on player order, seating or left/right neighbours (the "Player order / seating" group in `review-groups.json`, which includes "each player chooses / discards / returns" effects) are reviewed manually: the reviewer found them complicated in both formats. Do not draft notes for them; they are left out of the batches.

## Format
- One to three short lines per note. Each line starts with "Ensure ..." and states a testable expectation.
- Two notes per card: **Twin Suns** (3-4 players, free-for-all) and **Always Two** (2v2 teams).
- If a line depends on a rule the sources do not settle, start it with "Open:" and state the question. Never guess a ruling.
- Do not state rules you have not confirmed in the sources (CR v7.0, `format-rules.md`, `archive_old/rules-rulings.md`).

## Judge rulings to apply (Anthony, 2026-11-18; full table in `format-rules.md`)
- Twin Suns, "an opponent" in a static condition or comparison (no choice step): true if any one opponent qualifies. Abilities that choose "an opponent" when used still have the controller choose, and the choice persists.
- Always Two: the same conditions use either enemy individually (nothing summed across the two enemies); the teammate is never an opponent; the controller may choose either enemy.
- Always Two reveal is seen by everyone; "look at" is private; disclose uses only your own hand and aspects.
- Always Two: "a unit" / "a base" without "enemy" can target the teammate's cards; attacks only ever target enemies.
- Always Two: "you" is personal; "have the initiative" is the team's.
- Twin Suns, "the player to their right": from each player's own perspective it is the player before them in turn order (R7).
- Always Two seating: the two teams sit across from each other (teammates adjacent). "The player to their right" is a teammate for one player on each team and an enemy for the other (R8).
- Choices made in turn by each player: player order is always clockwise. If the card gives no other start (e.g. Merciless Contest), it starts with the player who played the card. "In player order" starts with the player to the active player's left (R9). In Always Two this follows the seating in R8.
- Ruled (R20, confirmed 2026-11-24, both formats, not only Pilots): an ability or keyword that a card grants to a unit is used by that unit's controller, so "you" / "your base" in the granted text means the controller of the card that has the ability (the teammate, for a teammate's unit; the Vehicle's controller for what a Pilot grants). Do not raise an "Open:" line for this.
- Always Two base damage (R10): a base has a health total and damage counts UP on it; it can exceed the health, and the player is not eliminated. Never describe base health as "HP counting down to 0". Paying damage-to-your-base costs and receiving damage on a base at or past its health are still allowed.
- Always Two "each player" includes the teammate; "when an opponent ..." effects are not triggered by the teammate (R11).
- Always Two "a player" (not "an opponent") can be any player: an enemy, the controller or the teammate (R12).
- Always Two "if you've (done X) this phase": counts only what you or your own cards did, not the teammate's (R13).
- Always Two "an opponent ... their (nth) card": counted per individual enemy player, not per team (R14).
- Always Two Exploit can only use the player's own units, never the teammate's (R15). This is an engine-level rule: do not write per-card notes about it.
- "An opponent ... their previous action": that opponent's own most recent action this phase, per individual player in both formats (R16).
- Comparisons or totals over "enemy units" (e.g. "highest cost among enemy units") pool all opponents' units together in both formats (R17).
- "If [condition] an opponent, [effect on] that opponent": the controller chooses an opponent for whom the condition is true, and the effect hits that opponent (R18). A condition with no "that opponent" follow-up is true if any opponent qualifies (R1).
- Restore always heals the base of the controller of the unit that has it, however it was gained, in both formats (R19). Engine-level: do not write per-card notes about whose base is healed.
- Always Two "friendly resource" includes the teammate's resources: they gain granted abilities and can be readied; the teammate still pays only with their own resources (R21). A card that defeats or otherwise acts on a "friendly resource" (Sundari Palace, Enterprising Lackeys, Wrecker) can take the teammate's resource too, even as a cost or downside; the only thing a player cannot do is spend the teammate's resources to pay costs (R46). Do not raise an Open line about it.
- Always Two, a card that makes you use a teammate's unit's When Defeated / When Played ability: "you" is you, the player using it (R25). An ability granted to a unit is different: "you" is the unit's controller (R20).
- "Ready a resource" / "ready N resources" with no qualifier can ready any player's resource: your own, the teammate's, or an opponent's (R26). "Friendly resource" follows R21.
- Always Two: "take control of a unit" with no "enemy" can take the teammate's unit; it moves to the player who played the card and the owner regains it as the card says (R27).
- Always Two: a base at or past its health has 0 remaining HP, can still be dealt damage, and still triggers "deals combat damage to a base" (R28).
- Several "enemy" cards chosen/revealed (e.g. 3 enemy resources) can come from any combination of opponents or enemies; "its controller" is each card's own controller (R29).
- "Choose any number of players / opponents / bases" allows any subset (none, one, several, all), including the controller and the teammate where "players" allows; both modes must support every combination (R30). Mention this requirement in the notes of these cards.
- Any card that initiates an attack gets this Always Two line: "Ensure the attack can only be initiated with a unit you control, never with the teammate's unit." (No shared control.)
- Always Two Coordinate counts only units controlled by the same player, not the team (R31), and Sentinel (shared by teammates in Always Two) is tested as described in `engine-test-notes.md`. Both are engine-level: do not write per-card notes about them.
- Unqualified "a base" (no "enemy", "friendly", "your" or "defending") can target any base in play, in both formats (R32). Do not write a line saying so ("can be your own, the teammate's or either enemy's"); mention a base only when the card does something specific with it (remaining-HP condition, elimination, who draws, the defending player).
- "Another unit", "another Unique unit", "a unit" and similar with no control qualifier need no note: any unit can be chosen exactly as in 1v1, which the existing 1v1 suite covers (R33). Do not write a line saying the target "can be a teammate's or an opponent's unit". Write a note only when the card names who controls the card or does something 1v1 cannot show.
- Always Two: an upgrade can be played on any unit, including the teammate's, unless the card restricts it ("Attach to a friendly unit"). A discount applies to whoever plays the card unless the text says "you" (Guardian of the Whills): then an upgrade the teammate plays does not count. Do not raise an Open line about this (R34).
- Choosing "an enemy unit": write only the Always Two line "Ensure only an enemy's unit can be chosen, never the teammate's." Do NOT write a Twin Suns line saying the unit can be chosen from any opponent's units; Anthony decided it is irrelevant (R35). Keep Twin Suns lines that say something more, such as who decides or whose hand is affected.
- Standard lines for qualifiers (decided with Anthony, 2026-11-19), added to every card that has the qualifier unless the card's notes already say it:
  - "A / another / target friendly X" (one friendly unit, upgrade or token chosen): Always Two "Ensure the choice can be the teammate's X as well as your own." No Twin Suns line.
  - "An opponent" chosen by the ability (not a static condition or trigger): Twin Suns "Ensure the controller chooses which opponent, and the choice persists through the ability." Always Two "Ensure the choice is either enemy, never the teammate."
  - "Each / all enemy X": Twin Suns "Ensure every opponent's Xs are affected." Always Two "Ensure both enemies' Xs are affected and the teammate's are not." (Not for "choose 2 enemy units" or "up to N enemy units": those are chosen, from any combination of enemies.)
  - "Each / all friendly X": Always Two "Ensure the teammate's Xs are affected too." No Twin Suns line.
- Always Two: a Pilot (upgrade or deployed leader with Piloting) can be attached to a teammate's Vehicle. Do not ask whether it is allowed (R36). The player who plays or deploys the Pilot controls the upgrade, and the Vehicle keeps its controller (R36). Do not ask who controls the Pilot. What a Pilot grants to the Vehicle belongs to the Vehicle's controller ("you" is the controller of the card that has the ability, R20); the Pilot's own text uses the player who plays or controls the Pilot. Do not raise an Open line for either.
- Twin Suns: when a player is eliminated, every card they own is removed from the game immediately, even if another player controls it (R37). Do not ask what happens to a card owned by the eliminated player. Questions about a card the eliminated player only controls, about effects that resolve mid-way, or about leaving play stay open.
- Twin Suns: cards removed from the game by an elimination are not counted as leaving play (R38). If an effect eliminates a player partway through, the rest of the effect that refers to that player's cards fizzles (R39); other effects are decided card by card, so keep any unsettled one as an Open line.
- A resolved effect (When Played, On Attack, an event) that checks "an opponent" in an "if" condition: the controller chooses which opponent (Always Two: which enemy, never the teammate) the condition is checked against, and may choose one for whom it is not met (R40). Do not write "true if any one opponent qualifies" for these. Constant abilities ("While an opponent...", discounts, enters play ready) have no choice: they apply for as long as any one opponent qualifies (Always Two: either enemy), checked individually per opponent (R45). Ignore the official card rulings that say the controller chooses for such cards (Corrupt Politician, Dedra Meero): the judges decided they apply only to triggered abilities.
- In Twin Suns never write a line that only says something comes from or goes to the controller's own hand, deck, resources or base ("the unit is played from your own hand and the damage goes to your own base"); it is obvious from the card text.
- Never write filler such as "Ensure it behaves as in 1v1; no format-specific case": if a card needs no multiplayer test, leave the note empty. Do not write a line about an unqualified "a unit", "any unit", "a base" or "your hand/your base" in Twin Suns (R32, R33); "your base" lines stay for Always Two only until the code scan.
- Plot cards: do not write any line about when the Plot play is available or whose leader deploy or resources it uses. It is tested once under the keyword in the engine notes. Only write what the card does beyond Plot.
- Fortify upgrades (attached to your own base): do not write "can only be attached to your own base" on any card. It is tested once under the keyword in the engine notes. Only write what the card does beyond that.
- Reveal / look at / disclose: who sees the information is tested once as an engine check, not per card. Do not write lines saying a reveal is seen by everyone including the teammate, a look at is private to the controller, or disclose uses only your own hand and aspects, never the teammate's. Still write lines for what is unusual about the card: which opponent or enemy's hand or deck is affected and the choice of it, a reveal that changes what later steps do, a deck or hand that is not the controller's, and so on.
- Bounty: who collects it is tested once under the keyword (R41). A Bounty is always collected by an opponent of the player who controls the unit; if the controller (or, in Always Two, the controller's teammate) defeats it, the defeating player chooses which opponent collects it. Do not write lines about who collects a Bounty, that other opponents or the collector's teammate do not collect it, or that a controller-caused defeat has the controller choose. Still write lines for what the card does beyond that (an "if an opponent controls a unit with a Bounty" condition, a granted Bounty, ownership versus control, the unique check) and keep real Open lines.
- Win / lose the game by a card (R48-R50): Twin Suns "you lose the game" is an elimination, "you win the game" ends the game at once; Always Two the result is the team's. Both are tested once in the engine notes: do not write per-card lines about them. "Play only as your first action" means the playing player's own first action. Whether a teammate's units block Confidence in Victory stays an open question for the app.
- An unqualified "a deck" / "a player's deck" choice (Lando Calrissian, Full Sabacc) needs no note in either format: any player's deck can be chosen, as with any unqualified choice.
- Never write a ruling number (R5, R13, ...) or the words "ruling" / "per Anthony" in a note, an "Open:" line or a "why": the numbers are only for this file and mean nothing to later contributors. State the behavior itself in plain words.
- Do not write an "Open:" line for any of the questions above; apply the ruling.

## Format reminders
- Twin Suns: "an opponent" is chosen by the player using the ability and the choice persists through the ability; "each opponent" is every non-eliminated opponent; eliminated players are not players for any effect.
- Always Two: a teammate is never an opponent; "friendly" includes the teammate; "you control" counts only your own cards; you cannot spend a teammate's resources, use their aspects, or attack with or play their cards. A base at 0 HP does not eliminate its player.
